Privacy & accessibility

A few things to know when using FixMyStreet.

Privacy

When you submit a report, we save the issue type, description, selected map location, and submission date. These reports can be viewed on the map, so please leave out names, phone numbers, and other private information.

Your browser only requests your location when you press “Use my location.” You can choose a point on the map instead.

The map uses OpenStreetMap. Some fonts, styles, and scripts also load from external providers.

Accessibility

You can use Tab to move between links, buttons, and form fields. Form fields have labels, and status messages are available to screen readers. You can also use your browser's zoom controls to make text larger.

If something is difficult to use, please let us know through our contact page.
