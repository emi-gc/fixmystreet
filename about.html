About FixMyStreet

FixMyStreet is a community reporting website made to help people share local street problems and support cleaner, safer neighborhoods.

What We Focus On

Simple tools that help people care for their streets.

Easy Reporting

Users can describe an issue and mark where it happened on the map.

Community Awareness

Reports help everyone understand which areas need the most attention.

Better Streets

Clear information can help communities push for faster and better repairs.
